The best cafés are decided at the table: where the light falls, whether the chair is comfortable, how long you can stay.

## Look for the window

Light is the first thing worth checking. A table by the glass turns a flat white into a still life without any effort.

## Count the plants

A café with plants is a café that someone cares about. Green on the shelves, green on the counter, green leaning in from outside.

## Order slowly

The pour-over takes four minutes. That is the point.
