The hour after dusk is when old streets change character. Shopfronts glow, lanterns come on and the crowds thin.

## Wood and warm light

Timber fronts take lamplight well. Everything goes amber, and the sky above stays a deep, clean blue for a few extra minutes.

## The pagoda on the hill

From a hillside, a red pagoda sits in front of the mountain like a full stop. Photographed early, the peak is pink behind it.
