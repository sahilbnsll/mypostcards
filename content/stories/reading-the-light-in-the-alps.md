Mountains are patient subjects. The light is what moves, and the trick is knowing where to stand when it does.

## First light

Before the sun clears the ridge, the valley fills with cloud. From above it looks like water that has forgotten to fall. The peaks stand out of it, pink for a minute, then white.

## The lake at midday

By noon the cloud is gone and the lake takes over. Limestone walls turn the water green-blue, and a single wooden boat is enough to give the whole frame its scale.

## After dark

When the last hut light goes off the sky shows its depth. A long exposure, a tripod on cold rock, and the galaxy lies along the ridge like a second mountain range.
